# Apiary calendar

The series follows the beekeeper's year: the topic of each episode fits the season.
Rule from Volodymyr: "орієнтуємось на графік робіт пасічника, а автоматизатор-сисадмін
завжди пристосується". Lyrical digressions are always allowed, especially in autumn and winter.

Facts come from Volodymyr's stories (see `stories/`). Fill in the other seasons as he tells them.

## Late autumn and winter (now: November 2026)

- Bees barely leave the hive: they fly only above ~12 °C.
- Already done by this point: treatment against mites (varroa) and diseases, enough food
  stores, hive insulation.
- The hive is not opened. Contact with the bees is "по вай-фаю" (remote monitoring).
- The beekeeper repairs equipment and makes new equipment.
- He analyses past seasons and plans the next one.
- The best time to "пізділінкати" and tell tales.

Characters in winter:
- Bees: inside the hive. Бзинька can still show up (she is a special case, a remote reviewer).
- Медунко: asleep in his den, sucking his paw. Can't beg for honey now.
- Колючка: asleep too.
- Сірий: visits Pylyp on quiet evenings.

IT parallels (for jokes):
- Mite treatment = security patching; feed stores = capacity planning; insulation =
  hardening; the closed hive = production you must not touch; Wi-Fi contact = monitoring.
- Season analysis = retrospective; planning the next season = roadmap.
- Repairing equipment in winter = the maintenance window.
