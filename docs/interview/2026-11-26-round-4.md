# Interview, round 4 (2026-11-26)

Started after Volodymyr called the project a mess and asked to work the way handmake does
(interview -> spec -> architecture -> tasks). Question 1: where this project ends and
postpilot begins.

## Volodymyr's answer (verbatim, dictated)

> ні не так . якщо ми не домовимося з постпілотом про публікування відео за нашими правилами,
> тоді будемо самі робити це. дай обгрунтування і виріши це зараз., це буде відправною точкою.
> я наприклад хотів би щоб цей проект був би фабрикою по виготовленню мультиків. задання
> отримував і погоджував через телеграм. а публікація то для постпілота.

## Decision (starting point for the spec)

1. **This project is a cartoon factory.** It takes tasks (ideas, tall tales, the monthly plan)
   from Volodymyr in Telegram, makes the episode, and gets his approval in Telegram.
2. **The factory has its own Telegram bot.** postpilot's bot stays postpilot's. Telegram
   bots are free, and the factory then waits on nobody's queue for its own workflow.
3. **Publishing is postpilot's job, under our rules.** The factory hands over a finished reel
   (DE video, cover, post text, release time); postpilot publishes it.
4. **Our publishing rules** (the contract postpilot must accept):
   - R1. The post text is posted **as written**, no AI rewrite (it was approved with the script).
   - R2. `cover.png` is the video thumbnail wherever the network allows (LinkedIn, IG, FB).
   - R3. Publishing at the given release time (Tue/Thu 08:00 Berlin), without a tap that hour.
   - R4. Machine handoff, no manual `/work` steps; the same reel is never posted twice.
   - Kept from today: LinkedIn DE, Threads, Instagram and Facebook Reels; AI label.
5. **Fallback.** If postpilot declines R1-R4, or has not shipped them by **2026-12-14**,
   the factory publishes by itself. Until then reels go through postpilot's manual `/work`,
   and Volodymyr replaces the generated DE text with ours using Edit in the preview.

## Why

- **Publishing is the hardest part to build, and postpilot already has it.** Working tokens
  for LinkedIn, Threads, Instagram and Facebook, the LinkedIn video upload, Reels with ffmpeg
  fitting, the AI label, idempotent posts and a Retry button. Doing it again means our own
  LinkedIn and Meta apps, their reviews and token renewal: weeks, and two copies to maintain.
- **R1-R4 are small for postpilot.** The `No description` path already skips the AI call
  (R1 is that path plus our text); `publish-due` already publishes at a set time (R3); the
  LinkedIn Videos API, IG `cover_url` and FB thumbnails exist (R2); ADR 005 already sketches
  the handoff (R4).
- **The rules are not negotiable, the tool is.** The cover rule and the post text are what
  reach costs us today (002: 25 impressions with a dark frame vs. 70 for the trial reel).
  A publisher that rewrites the text or drops the cover is no use to us, so there is a date.
- **The control channel is the factory's product.** Ideas in, plan and scripts out, silent
  approval: that is what this project is. Inside postpilot it was a second product in a
  foreign queue, and half of our roadmap was waiting there.

## Consequences

- `docs/postpilot-requests.md`: R1-R4 are filed as one request.
- ADR 005 (handoff) is revisited in the architecture: Telegram `file_id`s are per bot, so the
  handoff needs postpilot's bot token or another way to pass the video.
- Questions 2-6 of this round stay open (who writes the script, MVP, success numbers,
  portfolio, process).

## Question 2: who writes the script (answered 2026-11-26)

Volodymyr asked whether a session with Claude is paid from the subscription or the API.
Checked: Claude Code here is logged in with his claude.ai subscription (no API key), so
sessions cost nothing beyond the subscription limits; a pipeline calling the API would be
billed separately. Options given: in a session with him; the factory calls the API; the
factory runs Claude Code headless on the laptop with the subscription.

> добре, я триматим у включеним і мушу щось тицяти?

Answer given: nothing to tap; a systemd timer starts the factory (catches up after sleep),
Claude Code runs headless with permissions set in advance, re-login requests go to Telegram;
the laptop is needed anyway for voices and rendering; it must not sleep on a closed lid;
night runs share the subscription limits.

> приймаємо 2. решту питань висвітлиш у новому вікні

**Decision:** the factory generates scripts on the laptop with Claude Code headless on the
subscription (no API spend); Volodymyr edits and approves in Telegram. Generation runs at
night.

## Questions 3-6 (answered 2026-11-26)

Proposals given: (3) MVP = one full cycle in a week: idea in the factory bot, script at
night, approval in Telegram, DE render with cover and post, package to postpilot;
(4) three numbers after 8 weeks; (5) the repo proves an end-to-end automated business
process; (6) gates only.

> ідея чи побрехенька сама не приходить. я маю кидати їх у чат боту голосом або письмово.
> їдеї шикуються у черзі. чергу я можу правити і редагувати. якщо нема нічого в черзі, йде
> повідомлення мені, на яке я відповідаю ідеєю. якщо не відповів то ідею береш із
> запасників нашого проекту.далі за запропонованим тобою порядком. 4. згоден 5. давай так
> як ти кажеш. раніше ти видав думку що самі мультики і є частиною портфоліо. крім того ми
> маємо показати звязок і роботу двох проектів(постпілот) редмі всі пишуть англійською: ми
> чимось файніші чи мудріші(мулрість)? 6. давай ворота

**Decisions:**

3. **MVP: the idea queue plus one full cycle.** Volodymyr sends ideas and tall tales to the
   factory bot by voice or text; they line up in a queue he can view, edit, reorder and
   delete. When the queue is empty, the bot asks him for an idea; if he does not answer in
   time, the factory takes one from the project's reserves (`bible/`: ideas, gags,
   unused tales). Then: script at night, approval in Telegram, DE render with cover and
   post text, package to postpilot.
4. **Success after 8 weeks (about 16 episodes):** 16 of 16 released on time (Tue/Thu 08:00
   Berlin); on average at least 300 LinkedIn impressions per reel; at least 3 conversations
   about automation that started from a cartoon.
5. **Portfolio:** the repo proves an end-to-end automated business process (order ->
   approval -> production -> handoff to publishing) with working code, tests, CI, ADRs.
   The cartoons themselves are part of the portfolio (the README links them), and the repo
   shows how two projects work together: the factory makes, postpilot publishes, under a
   written contract (R1-R4). README: English is the main one (GitHub norm, readers beyond
   Germany), plus a short `README.de.md` for German clients, who are the target audience.
6. **Process: handmake gates only.** Interview -> spec -> architecture -> tasks, a short
   approval from Volodymyr at each gate, then small PRs merged on green CI.
