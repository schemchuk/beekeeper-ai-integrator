Questions: monthly plan approval and what happens when he does not answer in time;
how success is measured.

## Extracted requirements

- **Monthly plan, silent approval:** if there are no edits by one day before the approval
  deadline, the bot sends a warning: without edits by tomorrow morning the plan is accepted
  automatically. Then it is accepted.
- **Script, silent approval:** a script without edits by its deadline is accepted
  automatically, with no warning message.
- **Statistics:** roughly like postpilot (tracked short links per post and language, daily
  snapshots, a `/stats`-style overview). Details go to the product description.
- **Phrase (not a requirement):** "водити як гусака на мотузочку" = to strip someone of
  their will, impose and make them do your will. Saved to `voice/volodymyr.md` and as an
  episode seed in `bible/ideas.md`.
