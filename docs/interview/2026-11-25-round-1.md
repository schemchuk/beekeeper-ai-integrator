## Extracted requirements

- **Audience:** German-speaking first (job "for the soul" in Germany). Ukrainian market is
  a fallback from 2029. Mixing languages on one feed is assumed bad for the algorithms.
- **Main channel:** LinkedIn (his largest audience). YouTube Shorts: open question,
  maybe a separate project later.
- **Success:** a job as an automation specialist; a growing circle of interested people,
  possible future business partners.
- **Frequency:** start at 2 episodes per week, may grow.
- **His role:** feeds ideas and tall tales early on; reviews, edits and approves each
  script and a monthly plan. Channel for all of this: the postpilot Telegram bot.
- **Tone:** follow common rules, but right on the edge. Irony and kind smiles in every
  episode. Targets: bureaucracy, Jobcenter, clumsy HR, everyone who keeps people from
  living, finding work, working in comfort and being happy.
- **Constraints:** hardware is fine; small budget for a Claude subscription and API.
- **Languages:** production text is German, with a Ukrainian translation for him. He edits
  the Ukrainian text, and the edits must flow into the German production text. Goal:
  improve his German along the way.
